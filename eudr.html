Distribution

Built into the forwarder's workflow

The buyer of convenience is the party already processing the bill of lading. The economic cost can sit with the shipper; the operational burden should not.

01 · INTAKE

One shipment instruction

Forwarder or broker submits container, bill-of-lading and supplier references through the existing booking workflow.

02 · BIND

Plot → batch → container

Coordinates, batch ledger digest, footprint unit and transport hops are bound into one canonical evidence file.

03 · COMMIT

Freeze and anchor

SHA-256 chain entry plus public time anchor. Subsequent changes create a new version, never a silent edit.

04 · CLEAR

Export the filing fields

Importer receives mapped fields, evidence references and a public verification route. The operator files its own legal statement.
