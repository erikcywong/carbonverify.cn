CJV-MTH-2.0 · working specification

A claim checked against a version, not a website.

The method is public so that reviewers can test it, challenge it and reproduce its outputs. It defines records and evidence grades. It is not an accredited standard and confers no certification.

Twelve structural rules

The method in one page

RuleRequirement
01 · Boundary firstBoundary, functional unit and batch definition are fixed before evidence collection.
02 · Primary dataMetred share is calculated from declared primary-data rules, not estimated to reach a grade.
03 · AttributionOne owner, location and batch identifier unless a published allocation rule applies.
04 · Factor provenanceEvery emission factor carries source, geography, year and applicability.
05 · Grade arithmeticA ≥70%, B ≥45%, C below 45%; U is self-declared and not verified.
06 · Baseline co-signingA counterfactual baseline is fixed and co-signed before the improvement it measures.
07 · Hard gatesMissing calibration, unco-signed baseline or insufficient metred share stops issuance.
08 · AbstentionInsufficient evidence produces no unit and a written reason — never a softer conclusion.
09 · Canonical commitmentRecords use a published canonical serialisation and SHA-256 commitment chain.
10 · Public time anchorDaily Merkle roots receive a portable public timestamp proof.
11 · Visible revocationRevoked records remain visible with date and reason; history is never rewritten.
12 · Claim partitionEvidence statements, assurance reports and certification conclusions stay separate.

Conformance

Test the structure, not our adjectives

A conforming implementation reproduces arithmetic, grades, commitments and refusal behaviour.

canonical = batch | total_kgCO2e | per_unit | grade | method record = SHA-256(prev_hash || SHA-256(canonical)) root = MerkleRoot(records_for_UTC_day) proof = record + path + public_anchor(root) if metred_share >= 0.70: grade = A if metred_share >= 0.45: grade = B if metred_share < 0.45: grade = C if hard_gate_failed: output = ABSTAIN

What conformance does not mean

  • It is not accreditation or recognition by HKAS, CNAS, IAF or APAC.
  • It is not evidence that a reduction claim is effective.
  • It is not a guarantee that the measured party told the truth.
  • It is not a certification conclusion.
Known limitations

Published with the method

Method choice affects comparability. Public anchors prove time and integrity, not truth. Remote evidence has different coverage from site evidence. Related-party records cannot establish independence. Regulatory acceptance remains with the relevant authority.
