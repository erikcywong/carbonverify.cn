The verified unit

The smallest claim anyone can check.

A carbon claim is only as strong as its smallest auditable unit. Ours is one production batch: measured, bounded, attributed, committed and recomputable. It is evidence — not an offset, credit or certification.

Five properties

Fail one, and it is not a unit

We do not issue a weaker version of the same product when evidence is missing. We record a refusal or a lower evidence grade.

Commercial structure

A label is not paid for twice by accident

The supplier pays for measurement and site verification. The buyer pays for a comparable, usable evidence unit. Public recomputation is free.

Where a licensed partner's conclusion is needed, partner review is scoped by shipment or quarterly evidence bundle. We do not simulate hundreds of low-value legal signatures just because a site produces hundreds of batches.

  • A batch-level product carbon evidence record
  • Comparable within the same method version
  • Revocable, with a visible reason
  • Independently recomputable without an account
A unit is not
  • A carbon credit, offset or traded instrument
  • A certification conclusion or regulatory approval
  • Comparable across different methods
  • Proof that a company or product is “zero carbon”
