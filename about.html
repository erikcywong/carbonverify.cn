What we are building

The evidence layer underneath the signature

Measurement, evidence grading, shipment documentation, cryptographic commitments and public verification are useful before — and independently of — our own accreditation.

Where a buyer requires an accredited certification conclusion, that conclusion comes from an appropriately accredited body working to its own scope and liability. Our role is to make the underlying evidence cheaper to review and harder to challenge.

STANDING RULE

Not yet accredited means not yet accredited

We will not remove that caveat to make a page look finished. A buyer who checks an accreditation directory and finds nothing is worse off than one who read the limitation first.

Two tracks

Sell evidence now. Earn signature authority separately.

Revenue from evidence products does not depend on the outcome or timing of an accreditation application.

TRACK B · OPERATING MODEL

Partner signature, our evidence

We supply measurement structure, ledger, grades, commitments and public verification. An accredited partner reviews and signs only within its own accredited scope. Signature work is bundled by shipment or review period, with responsibility priced honestly.

TRACK A · STRUCTURAL OPTION

Infrastructure first, body second

The default end state is a methodology and public registry business. A separately governed certification body is considered only where the right to sign justifies its governance, insurance and loss of unilateral control.
