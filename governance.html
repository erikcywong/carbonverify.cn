Independence is a structure

Built so the evidence can say no.

No sales override, no outcome fee, no silent grade change and no deletion of an inconvenient record. Independence depends on who can stop a decision — not on the word “independent” in a brochure.

Controls

Seven operating controls

  1. No equity or outcome commission

    No equity in verified parties; no share of premium, carbon price, loan approval or claim outcome.

  2. Fixed, published fees

    Price depends on scope and evidence work, never on the desirability of the conclusion.

  3. Sales cannot alter findings

    Scope, findings and technical review sit outside commercial reporting lines.

  4. Technical veto

    The independent reviewer can stop issuance without commercial override.

  5. Consulting ban and cooling-off

    No advisory work for a client we verify; two-year cooling-off after relevant prior roles.

  6. Quarterly conflict review

    Conflicts are recorded and reviewed by the impartiality committee.

  7. Refusal is documented

    The file records what was missing. Refusal is an output, not an embarrassment.

RELATED PARTIES

Disclosure beats a narrow disclaimer

CoffeeJeV is founder-affiliated. Any related-party record is labelled, technically separated and excluded from independent validation counts. We do not describe that work as third-party proof of independence.

Directors and staff disclose relevant roles, financial interests and supplier relationships annually and when circumstances change.

Records

Old work must remain defensible

Working papers, sampling evidence and technical reviews are retained for at least seven years, or longer while a label remains valid.

The most common independence failure is convenience: helping a friendly client prepare data and then verifying that help. We do not take those engagements.
