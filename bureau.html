Non-negotiable separation

The rating entity cannot be the data seller.

Commercial benchmarks operate through a legally separated data business with its own governance, revenue and disclosure. The verification entity does not receive a share of benchmark sales tied to grades or outcomes.

STRUCTURAL CONTROLS
  • Separate legal entity and accounts
  • No grade-change or outcome commission
  • Minimum cohort sizes and anonymisation
  • Published inclusion and exclusion rules
  • No issuer access to confidential case files
