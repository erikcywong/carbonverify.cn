Supplier journey

Free entry, event-driven graduation

  1. Seed · free and deliberately limited

    One site, two grade-U batches per year, method templates and public listing. No citable verification statement.

  2. Grow · remote evidence review

    Triggered by real events: a third batch, a second site, a citable buyer request or a compliance pack.

  3. Verify · site-level evidence

    On-site or remote verification supports A/B evidence grades where the measured share justifies them.

  4. History survives an upgrade

    The chain and evidence record are never reset. Portability remains available; the switching cost is history, not an exclusivity clause.
