Who pays

The measured party is not the customer

A supplier paying for its own favourable evidence creates the wrong incentive. The party with a reporting, financing or clearance obligation pays.

  1. Suppliers enter free

    One site, two self-declared batches per year, method templates and a grade-U record. Free is permanent, deliberately non-citable and never represented as verified.

  2. Buyers purchase comparable evidence

    Brands, importers, lenders and insurers pay for evidence they can use in scope-3 reporting, trade documentation or underwriting.

  3. Signing is bundled, not multiplied per batch

    Where countersignature is required, it is scoped by shipment or quarterly review bundle — not sold as hundreds of ¥40 legal signatures.

  4. Refusal is a billable technical outcome

    If the data cannot support the requested grade, we issue a written refusal and bill only for work performed. No one buys a softer conclusion.
