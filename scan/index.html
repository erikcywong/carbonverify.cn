What this is not

This record is evidence, not an offset, credit or traded instrument. It is not a certification conclusion, not regulatory approval, and not proof that a product or company is "zero carbon". It is not accredited by HKAS, CNAS, IAF or APAC. A regulated certification conclusion can only come from an appropriately accredited partner. Self-declared (grade U) records are entry records only — not verified, not externally citable.
