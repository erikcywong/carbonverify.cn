Case 01 · Coffee

From a plantlet to a financing decision

Seven links, with one evidence chain and no claim that a footprint alone proves agricultural quality.

LinkWhat must be provedEvidence supplied
01 · Plantlet productionBatch output, nursery energy and functional unitMeter stream, batch record, kgCO₂e/plantlet
02 · InputsMedia, nutrients and water within the declared boundaryQuantity ledger and factor provenance
03 · Farm transferBatch identity survives transport and mixing rulesAttributed handoff and exception record
04 · CultivationYield, energy and allocation under the same methodMetred share and evidence grade
05 · ProcessingWet/dry process energy and batch mass balanceBatch ledger and boundary reconciliation
06 · ExportPlot, batch and container references agreeEUDR shipment pack and public anchor
07 · FinanceThe lender knows which evidence it relied onPortable record, grade and revocation status

The evidence layer supports the claim about measured carbon and traceability. It does not certify cup quality, farm profitability or a borrower's creditworthiness.

Case 02 · Insurance

Underwriting the cost of being wrong

Evidence grade can be a rating factor because it describes how much of a claim is measured and how easily it can be reconstructed. It is not a direct estimate of loss.

A portfolio model needs historical revocation, dispute and severity data before a rate is credible. Until then, a grade-based price is a hypothesis, not actuarial evidence.

InputUse
Evidence gradePrimary-data coverage indicator
Revocation historyFrequency signal by method and sector
Exception recordsData-quality and control weaknesses
Public anchorsTime and integrity of the relied-upon record
Claims outcomesSeverity calibration over time

We charge flat data or assurance fees. No commission on underwriting or claims outcomes.

Case 03 · Beverage

One bottle across four countries

A consolidated claim must show where primary data ends and secondary factors begin.

INGREDIENTS

Origin batches

Supplier records, allocation and evidence grades for each material ingredient.

MANUFACTURE

Site energy

Metered production energy, output and batch mass balance at the bottling site.

COLD CHAIN

Transport hops

Route, temperature class, storage time and container references across borders.

PUBLIC CLAIM

Qualified wording

A bottle-level statement only as broad as the boundary and evidence coverage allow.

If the bottle is marketed as “climate neutral”, the footprint alone is insufficient. Claims assurance tests the additional wording, instruments and substantiation; the evidence layer does not silently turn into an offset provider.
